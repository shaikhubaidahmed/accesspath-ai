# Three-minute demo script

Target length: **2:45 to 2:55**. Record at 1080p with browser zoom at 100%. Use the stable **Outage demo** mode for the main story; show live mode only if it is healthy immediately before recording.

## 0:00 to 0:20: the friend and the promise

**On screen:** AccessPath hero, then the planner heading.

**Say:**

> I built AccessPath for [real relationship and first name, with permission], who [one truthful access need]. A route marked accessible is not enough when one broken transfer lift can end the journey. AccessPath checks every required link, shows the evidence, and prepares a backup without weakening the rider's needs.

Replace the bracketed text before recording. Do not state a medical detail or quote without permission.

## 0:20 to 0:48: define the hard constraint

**On screen:** Grand Central-42 St to Astoria Blvd, Step-free checked, rider note visible, Outage demo selected.

**Say:**

> This demonstration uses official MTA station, equipment, outage, and availability data. Step-free travel is a hard graph constraint. The language model interprets this note and explains the result. Deterministic graph search and official evidence decide whether the route is accessible.

Click **Find my safest route**.

## 0:48 to 1:18: show the reroute

**On screen:** route visualization and outcome bar; scroll to the side-by-side comparison.

**Say:**

> The shortest original journey transfers at Queensboro Plaza. The demonstration injects one clearly labelled transfer-lift failure. AccessPath rejects that path and reroutes through Times Sq-42 St while preserving step-free access. Plan A and Plan B remain side by side, so the change is understandable instead of hidden.

## 1:18 to 1:48: prove the evidence contract

**On screen:** evidence ledger, simulation badge, source URL, observed time, freshness, and confidence.

**Say:**

> Every important claim carries its source, observation time, freshness, and confidence. This orange item says simulation and links to the exact local scenario record. Live mode uses the current MTA feed; replay mode keeps the judging path reproducible. A prediction is labelled as a forecast, never as a fact.

## 1:48 to 2:12: show accessibility and the agent trace

**On screen:** click **Listen to briefing**, then open **Inspect this run**.

**Say:**

> The same verified facts become a spoken briefing through ElevenLabs when configured, with browser speech as fallback. The Mastra trace exposes five typed steps: interpret needs, resolve evidence, plan, explain, and enrich. Each reports its duration and whether it completed or used a fallback.

If ElevenLabs has not been activated, say "browser speech fallback" and do not claim ElevenLabs.

## 2:12 to 2:38: partner depth

**On screen:** technology grid, then brief cuts to real partner artifacts only.

**Say, keeping only proven clauses:**

> Gemma keeps note interpretation on private open-model infrastructure. TabPFN scores historical lift risk on a chronological holdout. Tinker measures whether a small accessibility-specific fine-tune improves structured extraction. Temporal durably rechecks the trip before departure. Sentry traces the workflow, while MongoDB and Tiger Data preserve the plan and time-stamped evidence.

Delete any sentence whose partner proof is not captured. Show metrics or dashboards, not source files alone.

## 2:38 to 2:55: close on the human outcome

**On screen:** recommended route, public URL, repository link.

**Say:**

> [First name] said, "[short verified reaction]," after trying it. AccessPath does not promise that infrastructure will stay working. It gives the rider a traceable plan, a backup, and a clearer decision before leaving home. The project, data pipeline, tests, and deployment are open source.

If the friend has not tested it, replace the quote with: "The next validation step is testing this with the friend it was built for." Never invent feedback.

## Recording checklist

- [ ] The real friend detail is approved.
- [ ] No placeholder text appears on screen or in narration.
- [ ] Scenario mode is visible before the simulated outage appears.
- [ ] The simulation badge and timestamp can be read at 1080p.
- [ ] The final public URL works in a signed-out browser.
- [ ] Every spoken partner name has a real artifact in the recording.
- [ ] Captions are added and checked manually.
- [ ] The final video is under three minutes.

## Backup plan

If live MTA or a partner API fails during recording, do not hide the failure. Use replay mode, state that the external service is unavailable, and show the visible fallback state. The core judging sequence requires only the checked-in replay and scenario evidence.
